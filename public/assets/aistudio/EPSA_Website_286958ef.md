## EPSA is now a full member of IPSF

In 2026, the 72nd IPSF General Assembly accepted EPSA, Ethiopia as a full member of the International Pharmaceutical Students’ Federation and welcomed us to the Federation. Full membership gives Ethiopian pharmacy students a stronger voice and wider opportunities in global health.

## Who we are

EPSA represents pharmacy students across Ethiopia and works to empower them. Our aims, objectives, and core values shape every action we take.

QualityTrustIntegrityPerseveranceCompassionRespectLeadershipExcellenceAdvocacy

### Our aims

- Represent all pharmacy students in discussions and decisions with professional, governmental, and non-governmental organizations.
- Give members a platform to plan and run their own extracurricular activities.
- Collect information on pharmacy education and suggest improvements toward unified education nationwide.
- Advocate on education quality and issues facing the profession.
- Support chapter offices in running accredited projects, AMR Day, and World Pharmacists Day.

## Looking ahead

EPSA runs accredited projects such as the clinical skills event, patient counseling event, leadership development program, and article writing event. We are also building a student exchange program and an EPSA Mega E-Library for lecture materials, exam resources, and research.

## What we advocate for

EPSA speaks for students on the issues that shape their education and careers.

**Quality pharmacy education**A strong foundation for competent graduates and healthy communities.

**Doctor of Pharmacy program**Preparing problem solvers and leaders for patient care, research, and innovation.

**Paid internships**Paid fifth-year attachments that attract and reward strong students.

**Standardized exams**Fair, comparable testing with common exam blueprints.

**School accreditation**Clear standards that protect the quality and recognition of pharmacy programs.

**A Pharmacy Council**Licensure, a register of pharmacists, and a voice for the profession.

## How EPSA is organized

The national executive committee has seven voting members and two co-opted non-voting members, supported by six subcommittees and two project organizing committees.

Each of our 25 regional chapter offices has its own five-member executive committee, five subcommittees, and taskforces that run student projects locally. Members meet through a General Assembly at the national level and a Regional Assembly in each chapter.
